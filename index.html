Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

AI Resume Builder & ATS Scan ("we", "our", "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and display name via email/password, Google Sign-In, or Facebook Login.
  • Resume Data: Personal information you enter into your resumes (name, work experience, education, skills, etc.) is stored securely in Firebase.
  • Profile Photo: If you upload a profile photo, it is stored in Firebase Storage.
  • Camera Access: The App may request camera access solely for taking profile photos. We do not record or transmit camera data.
  • Usage Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how users interact with the App. This data is anonymous and aggregated.
  • Crash Reports: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs to improve app stability. These logs do not contain personal information.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. We do not store your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To create, store, and export your resumes
  • To perform AI-powered resume analysis and ATS scanning
  • To manage your subscription and premium features
  • To improve the App through analytics and crash reporting
  • To send notifications about your account (if enabled)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase with industry-standard encryption. We use secure HTTPS connections for all data transfers. Your resume data is private and accessible only to you.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, database, storage, analytics, crashlytics
  • Google Play Billing — Subscription payment processing
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • Groq AI — AI-powered resume analysis (resume content is sent for analysis)
  • Microsoft Clarity — User behavior analytics

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, solely to operate the App.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the App settings
  • You can export your resumes as PDF files
  • You can request a copy of your data by contacting us

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the App.
